# References

This is a working index of literature mentioned in the project's dialogues, memos, and essays. An entry records a discussion or citation, not necessarily that the original source has been checked in full. ## Identified works

- **Bird, Alexander.** *Nature's Metaphysics: Laws and Properties*. Oxford University Press, 2007. [Publisher page](https://academic.oup.com/book/8067). Used as the stated basis for the AI reconstruction of dispositional essentialism in [the imagined Bird dialogue](Dialogs/2026-11-01-an-imagined-conversation-with-alexander-bird.md). The dialogue is fictional; its replies are not Bird's words.

Mentioned in [the Kant dialogue](Dialogs/2026-10-30-kant-causation-and-predictive-world.md), [the causation memo](Memos/2026-10-30-causation-as-working-assumption.md), and [the essay on predictive experience](Essays/2026-10-30-the-world-we-keep-in-view.md).
- **Kant, Immanuel.** *Critique of Pure Reason*, Second Analogy of Experience (A189/B232 onward). Discussed in [the Kant dialogue](Dialogs/2026-10-30-kant-causation-and-predictive-world.md), [the causation memo](Memos/2026-10-30-causation-as-working-assumption.md), and [the essay on predictive experience](Essays/2026-10-30-the-world-we-keep-in-view.md). Translation and edition have not been specified.
- **Kant, Immanuel.** *Metaphysical Foundations of Natural Science*, Mechanics. Discussed in [the Kant dialogue](Dialogs/2026-10-30-kant-causation-and-predictive-world.md) and [the causation memo](Memos/2026-10-30-causation-as-working-assumption.md). Translation and edition have not been specified.
